About

Haohao Qiaoshi Liu is a writer-director, editor and colourist working between New York, Helsinki and Sydney.

Her work moves between narrative film, documentary, music and commercial projects, with a practice that carries from writing and direction through the edit and final colour.

Available for projects internationally.
