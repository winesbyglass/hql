Haohao Qiaoshi Liu is a writer-director, editor and colourist working between New York, Helsinki and Sydney.

Her work moves across narrative film, documentary, music and commercial projects. She carries projects from the written idea and direction through rhythm in the edit and the final image in colour.

Available for projects internationally.
